Chapter 2: Antivirals

Antivirals, as could be assumed by their name, treat viral infections. This includes common infections like COVID-19, HIV, and Influenza. They work to fight viruses through several mechanisms. The most common method is when the antiviral binds to the virus molecule's receptors, blocking its ability to enter healthy host cells and reproduce afterwards.

Some common types of antivirals include:

  • Tamiflu®
  • Relenza®
  • Rapivab®
  • Xofluza®
