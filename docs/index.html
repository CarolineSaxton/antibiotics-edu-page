Antimicrobials are defined as substances that kill and inhibit microorganisms to prevent infection and the spread of disease. There are 4 recognized types of antimicrobials: antibiotics which treat bacterial infections, antivirals which treat viral infections, antifungals which treat fungal infections, and antiparasitics which treat parasitic infections.
