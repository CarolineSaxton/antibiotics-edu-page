Introduction to Antimicrobials

Antimicrobials are defined as substances that kill and inhibit microorganisms to prevent infection and the spread of disease. There are 4 recognized types of antimicrobials: antibiotics, antivirals, antifungals, and antiparasitics. Each type treats a different kind of infection. If the wrong type of antimicrobial is taken for an infection, this is called misuse.
Misuse allows the microorganisms causing the infection to grow stronger and gain resistance to our treatments. This leads to superbug infections that are very hard to treat or manage, which can be very dangerous to individuals and whole populations.

This website is intended to be a quick follow-along reading guide for users hoping to understand a little bit more about antimicrobials. Greater public education on this topic is essential to creating better use of these drugs so they remain effective in modern medicine.
